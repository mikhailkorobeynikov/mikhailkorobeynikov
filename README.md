## About
 
Mikhail Korobeynikov is a design engineer working where product design meets code. He treats design as the shipped product rather than the mockup: interfaces that feel right in the browser, match the design precisely and behave exactly as intended.
 
His work combines product design for fintech, startups and creative web with his own R&D — interface and motion systems, frontend tooling and rendering, built in TypeScript and Go. Motion is designed in code, against real constraints, not in a separate tool. Based in Moscow, Russia.
